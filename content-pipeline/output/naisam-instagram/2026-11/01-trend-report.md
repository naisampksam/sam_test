Researched 12 Oct 2026. Sources: web search (last ~30 days), the page's Instagram
Insights (13 Jul – 11 Oct 2026) and competitor notes.

**What this report could and couldn't use:**
- **No YouTube data.** There's no `YOUTUBE_API_KEY`, so `yt_trends.py` didn't run.
- **No direct Instagram data.** Instagram has no public trends API and blocks automated
  browsing, so Instagram signals here come from web coverage and your own Insights.
- **Audio isn't covered.** Trending sounds change weekly. Check them in the app before posting.

## 1. Summary
- **Gulf business is the biggest timely opening.** About 20% of the audience is in the Gulf.
  Abu Dhabi now lets you start a business without an office, freelance licences are up 224%,
  and Dubai launched a programme for Indian startups that links to Kerala. These are new, local
  and very shareable among NRIs.
- **WhatsApp + AI for small businesses** is the practical marketing topic of the year in India.
  Meta launched Business AI in the WhatsApp Business app, and 91% of online Indian adults
  chat with a business every week.
- **November has four strong hooks:** Kerala Piravi (1 Nov), the Diwali sales season (6–10 Nov),
  Global Entrepreneurship Week (16–22 Nov) and Black Friday (27 Nov).
- **Keep doing warning and contrarian hooks.** Your 90-day data shows 11K shares against 16K
  likes, an unusually high share rate. Your best titles are warnings ("Your business will
  fail!", "Don't Copy Blindly").
- **Simple talking-head reels with subtitles are in fashion in 2026.** That's exactly your
  format, so the advantage now comes from the hooks and the topics, not production.

## 3. Winning formats and hooks
- **Your own data:** warning hooks ("Your business will fail!"), instructions ("Use Your
  Advantage!", "Don't Copy Blindly") and one-word curiosity titles ("STARTUP") lead your
  recent reels. Your share rate is very high, so write lines people want to send to a
  friend or business partner.
- **2026 platform pattern:** short reels with a strong hook, on-screen text that works
  with the sound off, simple talking-head style and recurring series
  ([Sociolyzer](https://sociolyzer.com/blog/instagram-reels-trends/),
  [ManyChat](https://manychat.com/blog/instagram-trends-for-creators-2026/),
  [NewEngen](https://newengen.com/insights/instagram-trends/)).
- **Trial Reels:** these show a reel to non-followers first. Use them to test 2 hooks on the
  same topic before posting to your grid ([CreatorFlow](https://creatorflow.so/blog/instagram-trends-2026-creators-marketers/)).
- **Series:** a numbered series (e.g. "Entrepreneurship Week: Day 1/7") builds return viewers.
