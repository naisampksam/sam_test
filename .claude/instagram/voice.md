## My positions

1. You don't need 50–100 pieces to start a clothing brand. Start with one
   piece, sell through POD or dropshipping, and go bulk only once orders prove
   demand. That way there's no dead stock.
2. Higher GSM doesn't automatically mean better quality. Fabric composition,
   knitting, finishing and stitching matter just as much.
3. Don't copy another brand's size chart. Fit decides whether the customer is
   happy, so use a chart built for your audience. Ours came from trial and
   error.
4. Embroidery isn't for everyone. It costs more and takes longer. Choose it
   when the brand needs to feel premium.
5. Not all DTF is the same. Print quality depends on the powder and the press
   temperature.
6. The founder's job is marketing and sales. Production, packing and shipping
   are ours.
