1: profit illa. Ads-num marketing-num panam baaki undaavilla.
2: quality kurayum. Fabric and printing quality kuranjaal customer randaamathu vaangilla.
3: ningalude brand-ne aalukal ormikkunnath cheap ennu maathram aayirikkum.

Ningal ₹199-nu vittaal, naale aarenkilum ₹179-nu vilkkum.
Aa race-il small brands-nu jayikkan kazhiyilla.

Even a single piece ningalude brand name-il nammal production cheythu tharum,
ennittu ningalude peril direct customer-nu ship cheythu kodukkukayum cheyyum.

Ningalude brand start cheyyan ee kaanunna WhatsApp number-il connect cheyyuka.
