# HAIKUFIT · brag plan

**Tone:** samurai movie trailer (cinematic preset): big type, hard sword-cut transitions, taiko and koto score.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30 fps, 23.3 s (35 beats at 90 BPM, one beat = 0.667 s). Every cut lands on a beat.

## What it is
HAIKUFIT (haikufit.in) sells Japanese warrior-inspired streetwear in India: heavyweight oversized tees,
each carrying a Japanese warrior idea and a 5-7-5 haiku on the back. The store has its own samurai
runner game, Dojo Run, where a score of 1000 wins ₹150 off.

- **For:** young Indian buyers who like anime, Japanese culture and streetwear with a story.
- **Sets it apart:** the haiku on every tee, and a store you can literally play.
- **Most impressive claim:** the opening line of a trailer is printed on a shirt. ("Seven times I fell /
  eight times I rose from the dust / still I stand again" is the back print of the Nana Korobi tee.)
- **Visual hook:** a black screen, a taiko hit, and the haiku as a movie's opening lines.
- **Real UI shown:** the actual Dojo Run game (filmed frame by frame from the theme), crossing 1000
  points at dawn, and the game's real "You unlocked ₹150 off" card.
- **Share caption:** "Seven times I fell. Eight times I rose. Now it's on the back of a tee."

## Angle
Cut it like a samurai film trailer. The first three cards are the haiku, read like a film's opening
lines. The third card reveals it's printed on a tee. Then the title (the brand), the warriors (the tees),
the trial (Dojo Run) and the reward. It ends on the store address.

## Visual identity (from the theme)
Ink #0E0E0E, washi #F3EFE6, shu red #C8102E. Oswald for headlines (the theme's heading face), Assistant
for small text (the theme's body face), Yuji Syuku brush kanji. Letterbox bars, film grain, sakura
petals and embers, and the red sun from the tees' own artwork.

## Music cue guidance
Synthesised in the project's world: taiko (low drum with pitch drop), shime-daiko (high drum),
koto plucks in D "In" scale (D, Eb, G, A, Bb), a low D/A drone, sword whooshes and blade rings tuned
to D. Everything sits on the 90 BPM grid from frame 0.
